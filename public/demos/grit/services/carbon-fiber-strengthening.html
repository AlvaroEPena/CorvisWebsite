Carbon Fiber Repair & Strengthening

Externally bonded carbon fiber systems that add structural capacity without adding weight.

Overview

Carbon fiber reinforced polymer (CFRP) restores or increases the capacity of damaged and under-designed members. Our crews prepare the surface, saturate and place the fabric, and verify bond so the strengthening performs as engineered.

Benefits and applications

Benefits

  • High strength with minimal added weight
  • Minimal disruption to traffic
  • Quick installation versus steel plating

Typical applications

  • Girders and beams
  • Deck soffits
  • Columns and piers
