Owner-led. Deck-focused.

Grit repairs and protects bridge decks for agencies and contractors who need the work done right and on schedule.

A new name with deck-side experience behind it

Grit was founded to do one thing well: repair and protect the bridge decks that agencies cannot afford to see fail.

We keep the company small enough that the owner is on the job and large enough to run multiple crews. Every project starts with a condition survey and ends with documented testing, so the owner knows exactly what was done and why.

Health and safety

What the work is built on

  • Durability first

    We choose systems for how they perform in year fifteen, not how they look at handover.

  • Plain communication

    Clear schedules, honest risk calls and daily updates, so engineers are never guessing.

  • Safety as a habit

    Nobody goes home hurt. Planning, briefings and stop-work authority are part of every shift.

  • Ownership

    If something needs fixing after we leave, we come back and fix it.

Capabilities

Equipment and capabilities

Surface preparation, material handling and placement all run on our own equipment.

  • Surface preparation

    Shotblasting, scarifying and milling units sized for deck work, plus dust control that keeps the site clean.

  • Mixing and placement

    Mobile continuous mixers and screeding equipment for polymer and UHPC overlays.

  • Spray and membrane

    Truck-mounted spray rigs and heated application kettles for waterproofing systems.

  • Testing and QC

    Pull-off bond testers, moisture and temperature monitoring, and daily record keeping.
