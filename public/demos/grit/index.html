Why agencies pick Grit

Four things we do differently, and the reason decks come back on time.

  • Proprietary equipment

    Our mixing, placement and finishing machines are built and maintained in-house, so output is predictable and breakdowns do not eat the closure.

  • Tight closure windows

    We plan around the lane closure, not the other way round. Fast-cure systems and staged crews turn weekend windows into finished decks.

  • Family-built accountability

    Owner-led and hands-on. The person who bids your job is the person who answers when you call during it.

  • Manufacturer-approved applicators

    Systems go down the way the manufacturer specifies, by trained applicators, with the paperwork to back every warranty.

Four stages, no shortcuts

Every deck follows the same sequence, so quality does not depend on who is on shift.

  1. Assess

    Condition survey, core samples and delamination mapping define the right repair.

  2. Prepare

    Hydrodemolition, shotblasting or milling creates a sound, profiled substrate.

  3. Install

    Overlays, membranes and coatings go down with proprietary equipment and strict QC.

  4. Protect

    Cure monitoring, testing and documentation hand the structure back ready for traffic.
