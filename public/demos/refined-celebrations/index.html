About Us

Every detail,
with intention.

Refined Celebrations & Co. is a boutique wedding, corporate, and event planning company serving Indianapolis and Salt Lake City. We offer comprehensive event planning and professional photography services for weddings, events, families, and couples.

Our planning services meet clients wherever they are in the process—full-service support, flexible partial planning, or remote guidance. We also provide custom invitation design, personalized stationery, and curated gift baskets.

Our photography approach is rooted in storytelling: genuine connection, natural movement, and thoughtful composition to create images that feel timeless, emotive, and true to each client's experience.
